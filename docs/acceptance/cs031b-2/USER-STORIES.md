## Scope and limits

One dated window, optional literal title keyword, at most 24 local hours, starts within 90 days, maximum 1–5 claims. Default maximum one and whole-day interpretation are visible before confirmation. No weekly recurrence, multi-day rules, skill/pay preferences or automatic claim execution. Rules do not reserve employee time or prevent a later manual assignment. Conflict and actual claim-count consumption belong to CS-031C; notifications belong to CS-031D. Manager approval remains required for future claims, not for saving a private availability preference. CS-018 remains last.

Natural-language checks are finite examples, not perfect interpretation guarantees. Employees must review the exact summary. A submission receipt records that a rule was saved; Saved availability shows current status. Physical device keyboard, VoiceOver and additional browser engines were not newly certified. Limits of 20 current/100 lifetime records bound stored history; cancelled records are retained. AI limits bound requests rather than precise dollar spend. Preview uses separate temporary fictional data; the user's existing Mac database is unchanged.
